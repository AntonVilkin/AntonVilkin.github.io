Anton digest

Anton digest is a personal tool made by Anton Vilkin for his own use only. It is not a public service, and other people cannot sign up for it.

What it does

A few times a day it makes a short text summary for Anton of his new messages and upcoming events, so he doesn't have to open every app.

  • It reads Anton's own Gmail (read-only) to list new emails: sender, subject, date and a short preview.
  • It reads Anton's own Google Calendar (read-only) to list his events and check whether meetings mentioned in chats are already in his calendar.

It cannot send, delete or change any email or calendar event. It only asks Google for read-only access (gmail.readonly and calendar.readonly).

Privacy

The data stays on Anton's own computer and is never shared or sold. Read the privacy policy.
